About Us

CareerCraft helps you turn your work experience, skills, and education into a clear, professional CV. Whether you are applying for your first job, looking for a new opportunity, or updating an older CV, CareerCraft guides you through the process one step at a time.

Enter your details, choose the kind of role you want, and CareerCraft can help organize your information into a polished draft. It can also help improve the wording of your experience so employers can quickly understand what you have done and what you can offer. You stay in control: review every section, make changes, and check that all the details are correct before using your CV.

You can start by typing your information or pasting details from your LinkedIn profile. CareerCraft uses what you provide to create a draft; it should never add jobs, skills, or qualifications you don’t have.

Our goal is to make CV writing feel simpler and less stressful. CareerCraft gives you a helpful starting point, so you can spend less time worrying about formatting and more time preparing for the opportunities ahead. All the very best!!!
